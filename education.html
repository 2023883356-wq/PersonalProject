My learning journey began when I was just five years old in 2009, where I first attended a small kindergarten in Klang Gate before my family moved permanently to Kuala Lumpur. Even at that young age, school felt exciting to me — a place filled with colours, songs and early lessons that slowly shaped how I understood the world. That was the beginning of everything: learning to socialize, follow routines and discover simple joys in studying.

2010

At six years old, I entered Prasekolah Bandar Tasik Selatan for a year and was placed in Kelas Sayangku. This was where I first learned basic reading, writing and counting, and where I started building friendships for the first time.
2011 - 2016

From age 7 to 12, I continued my primary education at SK Bandar Tasik Selatan for six years until UPSR. At the same time, I also attended religious school. I first studied at SRA Al-Syakirin for two years before transferring to SRA Al-Muqarabin, which was closer to home. Over those years, I completed both UPKK and UPSRA, balancing academics between national school and religious school.
2017 - 2022

During my teenage years, I studied at SMK Bandar Tasik Selatan from Form 1 to Form 5. These six years shaped me the most—filled with challenges, growth and unforgettable memories. At 15, everything changed when COVID-19 hit. Schools shut down, online classes began and life felt completely different. Because of the pandemic, my batch did not sit for PT3, and our SPM was postponed by a year. People often call us the “promax Form 5 batch” because of how unusual and challenging our schooling experience was.
2023 - 2026

After finishing SPM, I continued my studies at the diploma level. I chose to pursue a Diploma in Information Management at Universiti Teknologi MARA because I wanted a different learning environment — something more practical, less rigid than school and more aligned with discovering real working skills. Until today, I am still continuing this journey, learning new things every semester and shaping the future I want.

    FRIENDSHIPS

  • From kindergarten to university, I’ve never been someone with a large circle of close friends. I can get along with everyone, but the number of people who truly stay in my heart has always been small. My closest friends are the ones who have been with me since my early school days — from preschool to primary school, then to secondary school. We grew up together, faced different phases of life, and created memories that still mean something to me until today.
  • When I started my diploma journey, everything felt new. I met people from different backgrounds, with different attitudes and personalities. At first, it was challenging because I’m more introverted, but slowly I learned how to adapt. Being in university opened my eyes — I got to understand people better, see different sides of friendship, and learn who genuinely stays and who only appears during certain moments.
  • For me, friendships are like small chapters in life. Some people stay for years, some stay for a moment, but each of them leaves a memory that shapes who we are. Friends are not always meant to stay forever, but the moments we share with them — the laughter, the arguments, the unexpected adventures — these are the things that remain in our hearts.
  • I believe friends are part of our journey for a reason. They come into our lives to teach us lessons, to bring joy, and sometimes to help us grow stronger. No matter how short or long the friendship lasts, every memory has its own meaning.
