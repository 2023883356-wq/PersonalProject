ABOUT ME

Hi! I love being called Hanim . I am a November girl, born at a HKL , where I currently live. I am 20 years old and pursuing a Diploma in Information Management at UiTM , Rembau campus.
For my "Hobby", I enjoy staying at home alone watching dramas, but I also love exploring beautiful and peaceful places or visiting new locations to clear my mind. I am naturally curious but a bit introverted, preferring solitude as a way to find calm. When I set my mind on something, I always give it my best effort. People who know me describe me as straightforward and blunt, yet kind-hearted.
My mother is originally from Kelantan and my father from Kedah . Both of my parents are teachers who moved to Kuala Lumpur for work and have dedicated over 20 years to education, inspiring me with their perseverance and commitment. This website is my space to share my journey, interests, and the experiences that shape who I am today.

Life is more than studies and work — let’s dive into what I love to do in my free time.

MY HOBBY

For my "me time",I love watching dramas, whether Chinese or Korean. Watching dramas gives me a unique sense of comfort because I don’t have to overthink so i can just immerse myself in the story. I enjoy various genres, from thrillers and suspense to horror, romance, historical, and even fantasy dramas. While I don’t usually follow actors, there is one Chinese actor I really like, Rosy , not just for her roles but also for her personality and acting style.
Lately, I’ve developed an interest in hiking. Climbing hills and being surrounded by nature gives me a sense of peace as I breathe in the fresh forest air. It’s physically challenging, but the feeling of accomplishment is very rewarding. I’m also drawn to other adventurous activities, though I haven’t had the chance to try them all yet. Photographyhas become another passion of mine. Capturing the beauty of the places I visit allows me to preserve those moments and relive the atmosphere later. It’s my way of keeping memories and appreciating the experiences that bring me peace and happiness.
I love traveling to new spots and enjoying beautiful views, especially places connected to nature. Being by the beach, listening to the waves, or watching the sunset helps me feel calm and refreshed. I particularly enjoy the evening and nighttime because it brings a sense of tranquility to my mind.

Sometimes the simplest things bring the most peace — here are the tastes and places that make me feel at home.

FAVORITE FOOD & PLACES

My all-time favorite drink is matcha. I prefer matcha over coffee because it has a unique taste — slightly bitter, earthy, sometimes creamy and just the right amount of sweetness. Nowadays there are so many matcha variations like strawberry matcha, chocolate matcha, taro matcha and more, and I honestly enjoy trying all of them. I also love sweet things like chocolates, cakes, and desserts. , These treats never fail to lift my mood, and I never get tired of them.
Because of my obsession with matcha, my friends even call me a matcha hunter A matcha enthusiast who hunts for the best matcha experiences. Whenever a new matcha place opens near my area, I have to try it — a little FOMO moment, honestly. I enjoy exploring cozy cafés, open-space night cafés, and even small car-boot stalls around KL that sell drinks by the roadside. These kinds of places have their own vibe: quiet, peaceful, and perfect for night healing sessions.
These spots and flavors help me recharge. Sometimes I sit there with my friends, talking about life, sharing stories, and letting out all the feelings we kept inside — a simple kind of healing that feels just right.
